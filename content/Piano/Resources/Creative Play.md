## Improvisation and Creative Play

Not optional. Not a reward. A core musicianship skill.

### Minimum: once per week, 5–10 minutes

#### Beginner (Stages 0–2)
- Play a scale, then make up a melody using only those notes.
- Play a I–V–I progression in the left hand, hum a melody over it.
- Change one note in a piece you know. Does it still work? Try another.

#### Intermediate (Stages 3–5)
- Improvise a 4-bar question, then a 4-bar answer.
- Harmonize a folk melody in three different ways.
- Play a 12-bar blues: LH pattern + RH melody.
- Take a piece you know and change its character: major → minor,
  fast → slow, legato → staccato.

#### Advanced (Stages 6+)
- Improvise a prelude in the style of a piece you are studying.
- Realize a figured bass.
- Improvise a cadenza for a Classical sonata movement.
- Compose a short piece (8–16 bars) and write it down.

### Why this matters
- You hear harmony instead of just playing it.
- You recover from mistakes more easily because you can "compose your way out."
- You develop a personal musical voice, not just a reproductive one.
- It is the fastest cure for "practice feels mechanical."
