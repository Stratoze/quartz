# Daily Card

## Before I sit down — 10 seconds
1. What am I improving today?
2. How will I test it?
3. What will I do if the test fails?

## Menu
- [ ] Learning — new material
- [ ] Polishing — musical and performance work
- [ ] Technique — etude, scales, arpeggios, RCM tech
- [ ] Method book — one concept or short piece
- [ ] Reading — sight-read easy material, no stopping
- [ ] Ear — sing, identify, play by ear, harmonize
- [ ] Maintenance — Anki review, 5–15 min
- [ ] Fun — play something I love, no goals

## Minimum rules
- Sit down. Play something.
- Work on one thing with full attention.
- Rotate learning/polishing items every 5–8 minutes.
- Stop while still clean.
- Stuck 5–10 minutes? Move away and return later.
- Tired? Do the minimum. No guilt.

## When I do not feel like it
- After coffee, laptop, or brushing teeth, I sit at the piano.
- I will just play one scale. That counts.
- Missed a day? Fine. Missed three? Still fine. Sit down tomorrow.
- The plan does not punish me. It is there when I return.
- I am not “trying to practice.” I am a pianist. Pianists sit down.

## First-sitting checklist
- Bench height: forearms roughly parallel to the floor, wrists level with keys.
- Distance: elbows slightly forward of the ribs, not tucked in.
- Score at eye level, well lit.
- Metronome nearby but not necessarily on.
- Phone in another room, or set up to record.
- Pencil in reach.
- Pick ONE thing from the menu.
- 15 minutes is a complete session on day one.
